# Part-Time Work Platform Product Introduction

## 1. Product Positioning

The Part-Time Work Platform is a digital management system built for enterprise flexible staffing scenarios. It helps companies move the full lifecycle of part-time workforce operations online, including recruitment, application review, shift scheduling, attendance, payroll settlement, fund management, and platform operations.

The platform serves enterprises, part-time workers, and platform operators. It is suitable for industries and scenarios that rely heavily on temporary, short-term, or part-time labor, such as food and beverage, retail, exhibitions and events, logistics, manufacturing, property management, home services, and campus part-time work.

With the Part-Time Work Platform, enterprises can find suitable workers more efficiently and reduce manual management costs. Workers can find jobs, view schedules, check in and out, and withdraw earnings more conveniently. Platform operators can centrally manage enterprises, workers, job categories, and operational configurations.

## 2. Core Value

### 2.1 Cost Reduction and Efficiency Improvement for Enterprises

Enterprises no longer need to rely on WeChat groups, spreadsheets, or manual phone communication to manage part-time workers. From job posting and application review to scheduling, attendance, and payroll settlement, the entire process is completed online, reducing repeated communication and manual statistics work.

### 2.2 More Transparent Workforce Management

Job information, application status, schedules, attendance records, payroll details, and fund flows are all recorded online. Enterprises, workers, and platform operators can view data according to their permissions, reducing disputes and improving management transparency.

### 2.3 Better Worker Experience

Workers can use the mobile app to view nearby jobs, submit applications, check review results, confirm schedules, complete attendance check-ins, view income, and apply for withdrawals. The overall job-seeking and payment process becomes more convenient.

### 2.4 Support for Platform-Based Operations

Platform operators can centrally manage enterprises, workers, job categories, system configurations, and operational data. This provides a foundation for future expansion into city sites, industry-specific zones, enterprise packages, and data-driven operations.

## 3. System Features

### 3.1 Enterprise-Side Features

The enterprise side is designed for hiring companies and supports daily workforce management through both a mini program and a PC management portal.

#### Job Management

Enterprises can create, edit, publish, and close jobs, while maintaining job titles, work locations, hiring headcount, pay standards, working hours, and job requirements.

Key capabilities include:

- Publishing part-time jobs
- Editing job information
- Closing completed jobs
- Viewing job applications
- Copying job sharing links to invite workers
- Filtering and managing jobs by status

#### Application Management

Enterprises can view worker applications and review them.

Key capabilities include:

- Viewing pending, approved, and rejected applications
- Viewing basic worker information
- Approving or rejecting applications
- Viewing application counts and pending review counts by job
- Moving approved applications into the scheduling process

#### Scheduling and Attendance

Enterprises can assign shifts to approved workers and monitor schedule execution.

Key capabilities include:

- Creating schedules
- Viewing schedule dates, time slots, workers, and jobs
- Cancelling future schedules
- Viewing check-in, check-out, and absence statuses
- Viewing attendance correction requests
- Approving attendance correction requests

#### Payroll Management

Enterprises can view workers' working hours and payable wages based on scheduling and attendance records, and perform payroll settlement.

Key capabilities include:

- Viewing worker name, job, schedule date, and working time slot
- Viewing pay standard, working hours, scheduled wage, and payable wage
- Viewing check-in and check-out times
- Filtering records by settlement status
- Batch settling unsettled records
- Deleting unsettled records
- Preventing repeated settlement or deletion of settled records
- Supporting settlement rollback

#### Fund Management

Enterprises can view account balances and fund flows to understand their financial usage.

Key capabilities include:

- Viewing account balance
- Viewing recharge, settlement, deduction, and other transaction records
- Viewing payroll payment records
- Supporting financial reconciliation

#### Enterprise Account Management

Enterprises can manage internal member accounts and permissions, supporting collaboration across multiple roles.

Key capabilities include:

- Creating enterprise accounts
- Editing account display names and roles
- Resetting passwords
- Disabling accounts
- Supporting roles such as administrator, HR, operations manager, and finance

#### Enterprise Settings

Enterprises can maintain basic company information, authentication information, and system configurations.

Key capabilities include:

- Maintaining enterprise profile information
- Enterprise real-name verification
- Auto-approval configuration
- Auto-settlement configuration
- Enterprise address and location management
- Template management

### 3.2 Worker-Side Features

The worker side is designed for part-time workers and is mainly used for finding jobs, applying, viewing schedules, checking in and out, viewing income, and withdrawing earnings.

#### Job Search and Browsing

Workers can browse jobs on mobile, view job details, and apply.

Key capabilities include:

- Viewing job lists
- Viewing job details
- Filtering by job category
- Viewing salary, location, time, and job requirements
- Applying for interested jobs
- Viewing nearby jobs

#### Applications and Review Status

Workers can view the jobs they have applied for and their review status.

Key capabilities include:

- Viewing application records
- Viewing pending, approved, and rejected statuses
- Viewing job arrangement information
- Receiving application review result notifications

#### My Schedule

Workers can view their work arrangements and clearly understand work dates, times, and locations.

Key capabilities include:

- Viewing future schedules
- Viewing historical schedules
- Viewing job information and work location
- Viewing shift status
- Receiving schedule change notifications

#### Attendance Check-In

Workers can check in and out after arriving at work, creating attendance records.

Key capabilities include:

- Check in for work
- Check out after work
- View check-in and check-out times
- View working hour statistics
- Submit attendance correction requests
- View absence or abnormal attendance status

#### Income and Withdrawal

Workers can view income details and account balance, and apply for withdrawals.

Key capabilities include:

- Viewing income details
- Viewing settled wages
- Viewing unsettled wages
- Viewing account balance
- Submitting withdrawal requests
- Viewing withdrawal records

#### Personal Center

Workers can maintain personal information and account settings.

Key capabilities include:

- Viewing personal profile
- Maintaining name, gender, birthday, and other information
- Viewing real-name verification status
- Viewing message notifications
- Managing settings

### 3.3 Platform-Side Features

The platform side is designed for platform operators and is used to manage enterprises, workers, job categories, and operational configurations.

#### Enterprise Management

Key capabilities include:

- Viewing enterprise details

- Viewing enterprise verification information

#### Worker Management

Platform operators can view and manage worker users on the platform.

Key capabilities include:

- Viewing worker lists
- Viewing basic worker information
- Viewing worker verification status

Key capabilities include:

#### Platform Configuration

Platform operators can maintain operational configurations that affect global business processes.

Key capabilities include:

## 4. Typical Use Cases

### Use Case 1: Temporary Holiday Staffing for Restaurants

Before holidays, restaurant companies publish jobs such as waiters, food runners, and kitchen assistants. After workers apply online, the enterprise screens suitable candidates and assigns shifts. Workers scan a code or use location-based attendance after arriving at the store. The system automatically records working hours, and the enterprise can batch settle wages after the holiday period.

Applicable value:

- Quickly supplement temporary staff
- Reduce manual scheduling and statistics costs
- Improve payroll settlement accuracy

### Use Case 2: Short-Term Staffing for Exhibitions and Events

Event companies publish jobs such as reception, security, guidance, and booth setup before an event. The platform collects applications and arranges shifts across different dates and time slots. During the event, attendance records confirm actual attendance. After the event, wages are settled uniformly based on working hours.

Applicable value:

- Support short-cycle, high-volume staffing
- Manage multiple jobs and shifts in one system
- Make attendance and payroll traceable

### Use Case 3: Promotional Part-Time Work for Retail Stores

Retail companies publish jobs such as sales assistants, stock clerks, and cashier support for promotional campaigns. After workers apply, store managers review applications and assign workers to specific stores and time slots. The platform records attendance and payroll data, making centralized reconciliation easier for headquarters.

Applicable value:

- Support collaboration between headquarters and stores
- Centralize job, worker, and payroll data
- Support flexible staffing across multiple stores

### Use Case 4: Peak-Season Staffing for Logistics and Warehousing

Logistics companies publish jobs such as sorting, packing, and handling during major sales campaigns. The platform helps enterprises quickly recruit temporary workers and manage actual attendance through scheduling and attendance records.

Applicable value:

- Handle staffing fluctuations during business peaks
- Reduce temporary staffing organization costs
- Improve on-site workforce management efficiency

### Use Case 5: Platform-Based Part-Time Workforce Operations

Platform operators can connect multiple enterprises and a large number of workers, forming a regional part-time workforce supply-and-demand platform. Enterprises publish staffing needs, workers apply independently, and the platform centrally manages categories, users, enterprises, and operational strategies.

Applicable value:

- Support multi-enterprise and multi-worker operations
- Build a regional part-time workforce service network
- Provide a foundation for platform commercialization

## 5. System Architecture

The Part-Time Work Platform adopts a multi-end collaborative architecture serving enterprises, workers, and platform operators.

### 5.1 Enterprise Side

The enterprise side includes an enterprise mini program and an enterprise PC management portal.

- The enterprise mini program is suitable for business owners and store managers to handle applications, scheduling, attendance, and payroll matters at any time.
- The enterprise PC portal is suitable for HR, operations, and finance personnel to perform batch management, data viewing, and internal collaboration.

### 5.2 Worker Side

The worker side is mobile-first, enabling workers to find jobs, apply, view schedules, check in and out, and withdraw earnings at any time.

The worker side emphasizes simple operations, clear information, and fast workflows, making it suitable for high-frequency use by part-time and temporary workers.

### 5.3 Platform Side

The platform side is designed for operations managers to manage enterprises, workers, job categories, and platform configurations.

It helps operators understand overall platform operations and centrally manage enterprises and workers.

### 5.4 Backend Services

The backend is divided by business role into enterprise-side services, worker-side services, and platform-side services. Each service provides API capabilities around its own business scenarios.

This architecture enables different ends to iterate independently and supports future scaling according to business volume.

### 5.5 Data Center

The platform stores core data such as enterprises, workers, jobs, applications, schedules, attendance, payroll, and fund flows. This data foundation supports operational analysis, financial reconciliation, and business decision-making.

## 6. Product Highlights

### Closed-Loop End-to-End Process

The full process from job publishing to payroll settlement is digitized, preventing information from being scattered across WeChat groups, spreadsheets, and manual communication.

### Multi-End Collaboration

The enterprise mini program, enterprise PC portal, worker side, and platform side work together to meet the usage habits of different roles.

### Transparent and Traceable Data

Jobs, applications, schedules, attendance, payroll, and fund flows are recorded, making traceability and reconciliation easier.

### Suitable for Scaled Operations

The platform supports multiple enterprises, workers, job categories, and roles, making it suitable for long-term part-time workforce platform operations.

### Reduced Management Risk

The system restricts key operations, such as preventing settled payroll from being settled again or deleted, preventing arbitrary job deletion, and using account disabling instead of deletion. These rules reduce operational errors.
